Biography / 01

A practice shaped by image, atmosphere and human presence.

Leona Li is a photographer and visual storyteller working across fashion, portraiture, and commercial imagery.

Her practice explores the relationship between identity, atmosphere and human presence, creating visual narratives that connect aesthetic sensitivity with brand communication.

With experience in luxury fashion production and international creative environments, she combines visual sensitivity with practical production knowledge.
